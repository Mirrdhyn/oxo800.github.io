The craft

Infrastructure does not stop at the datacenter.

A rack in a machine room, a cloud account, an inference computer on a desk: three forms of the same work. Size it, wire it, automate it, supervise it, document it. The tools change every five years, the discipline stays the same.

Twenty years in the field behind this conviction: physical estates spread over two datacenters, complete migrations to the cloud, the configuration of several hundred machines industrialised through code. From the network to the container, by way of the databases and the front ends that must not go down.

Local AI extends that story. A model that runs at your place is memory, thermals, network and operations. We measure it on our own hardware, and we publish the measurements.
