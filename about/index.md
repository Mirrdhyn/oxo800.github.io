# About

0x0800 runs language models on hardware you can actually buy: Intel Arc Pro cards, DGX Spark, Macs. The rest of the time it is infrastructure work: build farms, backups, deployments.

Every number here links to its source: a commit, an issue, a measurement you can rerun. When an article is wrong, we fix it in the article and date the correction.

The name is the IPv4 EtherType: the two bytes that say what an Ethernet frame carries.
