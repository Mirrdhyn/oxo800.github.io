## 1. Purpose and scope

These general terms of sale govern the sale of hardware, deployment, training and support services, and the rentals offered by 0x0800 SRL ("the Provider") to its professional customers ("the Customer"). They apply to every order, to the exclusion of the Customer's purchasing conditions. They are reserved for business-to-business relationships.

## 2. Quote and order

Hardware and services are the subject of a nominative quote, valid for 30 days. The order becomes firm on signature of the quote or payment of the deposit. Prices are communicated by quote; only the accepted quote is binding.

## 3. Prices and payment

Prices are expressed in euros excluding tax; VAT and, where applicable, delivery and installation costs are added. Unless the quote states otherwise, a deposit is paid at the time of the order and the balance is payable 30 days from the invoice date.

Any late payment automatically bears interest at the rate provided by the Act of 2 August 2002 on combating late payment in commercial transactions, together with a flat-rate recovery fee.

## 4. Delivery, installation and acceptance

Lead times are indicative. Hardware is delivered and, where applicable, installed at the Customer's premises. Acceptance of the services is recorded by a signed report or, failing that, by actual commissioning. Risk passes to the Customer on delivery.

## 5. Retention of title

Hardware sold remains the property of 0x0800 SRL until the price has been paid in full, principal and ancillary amounts.

## 6. Rental and purchase option

Rental is concluded for a term of 24 months. Rented hardware remains the property of 0x0800 SRL throughout the term of the contract. At the end of the contract, the Customer may acquire the hardware at its residual value. The hardware is used with the care of a reasonably prudent person and returned in good condition, normal wear excepted. Updates and support are included in the rent.

## 7. Warranty

The hardware benefits from the manufacturer's warranty. 0x0800 SRL provides the statutory warranty applicable between professionals. Damage resulting from non-compliant use, modification or intervention by an unauthorised third party is excluded.

## 8. Deployment and training services

Services are provided according to the terms of the quote. Any cancellation or postponement of a training session less than seven working days before the scheduled date may be invoiced.

## 9. Support and subscriptions

Support subscriptions are concluded for the stated term, renewable. Termination is by written notice of one month. The level of commitment (response times, supervision) is defined by the offer subscribed.

The Hygur software is licensed under its own terms. The sale of hardware or of a service does not transfer any intellectual property rights. The Customer remains the sole owner of its data.

## 12. Liability

The liability of 0x0800 SRL is limited to the amount paid by the Customer for the order concerned. Indirect damages, notably loss of operations, data or revenue, are excluded, within the limits permitted by law.
